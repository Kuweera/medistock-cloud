Dosing rules

The app contains no dosing knowledge. A rule exists only if your own clinician or pharmacist enters it here, says where it comes from, and approves it. Orders for that medicine are then checked against it (and weight-based doses can be worked out from it). Medicines with no approved rule are not checked, and the screen says so.

Add a dosing rule

Required: the medicine, where the rule comes from, and the country. A rule must also have at least one dose limit, a dose per kilogram, a warning, or the high-risk tick. Everything else is optional.
