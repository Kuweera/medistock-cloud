Record triage / vital signs

Every box here is optional, but enter at least one measurement, a category or a note.
Fill in only what you measured. Each save is a new record, so you can repeat observations during the visit.
0 means no pain. 10 means the worst pain imaginable.
More measurements: blood glucose, consciousness, arm circumference
How awake the patient is. Alert: awake and responding normally. Responds to voice: wakes when spoken to. Responds to pain: reacts only to pain. Unresponsive: no reaction.
Mid-upper arm circumference: the distance around the upper arm, measured with a tape. Mostly used for young children.
Your own decision about how urgent this patient is. Emergency: needs help immediately. Urgent: needs to be seen soon. Standard: normal waiting. Low priority: can wait longer.

Add an order

For a medicine, required: the medicine, the dose, the unit, how it is given, when it starts, and the details of the chosen pattern. For a procedure, required: the procedure and when it starts. Everything else is optional.
Type or choose, for example by mouth, injection into a muscle, or into a vein.
Items used up during the procedure, for example gloves or a dressing.
Type a code such as BD, TDS or q8h. Its meaning is shown. It only fills in the pattern below. It never sets the dose.
Choose the pattern. A short explanation and an example appear below.
Enter the total in the smallest unit of the medicine, for example 20 for 20 tablets, or 100 for 100 ml. If you leave it blank, the pharmacist will enter it.
