## 4. Test pitfalls learned (for writing rules tests)
Call `ctx.firestore()` once per context. A cached authenticated client can mask a refusal (use a fresh `env.authenticatedContext(uid).firestore()`). A client `new Date()` can equal `request.time`. The first line of `firestore.rules` is asserted by `tests/integration.test.js`. The 1000-expression budget: put cheap discriminating checks first in every `allow update`. Denied cases printing "maximum of 1000 expressions" are harmless.
