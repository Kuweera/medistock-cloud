💰 Profit Summary

What you earned — counts every sale made in this period, whether it was paid in cash or given on credit.

Some sales have no recorded cost, so their cost is estimated from the drug's current wholesale price.

🧾 Cash & Inventory Movement

What actually moved in or out of your hand — includes old credit payments collected in this period, and new stock bought (even if it hasn't sold yet).

Why don't these two match?

Net Profit and Net Cash Movement rarely match exactly — that's normal, not an error. Common reasons:

① Sold on credit: counted as profit the moment you sell it, even though the cash hasn't arrived yet.

② Old credit paid off: cash arrives now, but the profit was already counted on the day of the original sale.

③ New stock bought: cash goes out now, but it doesn't reduce profit until that stock is actually sold.

④ Owner deposits/withdrawals, other income, cash surplus/shortage: these move real cash but aren't part of profit.
