📥 Import from MediStock Pro

Brings your whole MediStock Pro history into this app: drugs and stock, batches and expiry dates, sales and receipts, purchases, credit customers and their payments, expenses, cash records, stock-taking records and pick lists.

Before you start: make a backup on the Backup & Export page, stay online, and keep this screen open until it says Finished. It is safe to run again: records already imported are skipped, never doubled.

ℹ️ What is not copied

• Pro's logins and settings (shop name, phone) are not copied; this app has its own. • Pro's per-sale stock lines are not copied because every sale already carries them. • Pro never tracked what you owe suppliers, so there are no supplier debts to bring across.
